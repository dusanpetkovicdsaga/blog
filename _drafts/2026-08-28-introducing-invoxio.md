I’ve been working on a new project called **Invoxio**: a simple way to create professional invoices with AI.

<!--more-->

The idea is straightforward. You describe the client, the work, and the line items, and Invoxio turns that into a branded PDF invoice instantly.

## Why I Started It

Invoicing is one of those tasks that should be boring, fast, and reliable.

Instead, it often becomes a small pile of friction:

- opening yet another tool
- filling the same client details again
- tweaking layout just to make it look presentable
- exporting and sending the invoice manually

That is the kind of workflow I like to remove.

## What Invoxio Does

At a high level, Invoxio is built to help you:

- generate invoices from plain language
- keep the result clean and professional
- produce a branded PDF without extra steps
- reduce the time spent on repetitive admin work

The goal is not to replace accounting software or turn invoicing into a giant platform.
The goal is to make invoice creation feel quick and obvious.

## What I’m Optimizing For

I care about a few things here:

1. **Speed** - create an invoice in seconds, not minutes.
2. **Clarity** - no confusing UX or crowded screens.
3. **Presentation** - invoices should look polished by default.
4. **Focus** - one job, done well.

That tends to produce better products than trying to do everything at once.

## What’s Next

This is still an early project, but I want to keep pushing it toward something useful and real.
The next steps are mostly about tightening the flow, improving the output, and making the experience feel effortless.

If you want to follow along, the project lives at [invoxio.app](https://invoxio.app).
